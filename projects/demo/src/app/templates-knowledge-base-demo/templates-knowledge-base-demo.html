How to Reset Your Password

If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it in under a minute. From the login screen, select "Forgot password?" and enter the email address associated with your account. We'll send a secure, time-limited reset link to that inbox.

Once you open the link, you'll be asked to choose a new password. For security, it must be at least 10 characters and include a number. After saving, you'll be signed out of all other active sessions automatically.

If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or confirm the address on file under Settings → Account. Still stuck? Contact support and we'll help you regain access.
