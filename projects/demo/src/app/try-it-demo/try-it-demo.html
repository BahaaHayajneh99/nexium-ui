Try It

Write a component — a template and a TypeScript class — and see it rendered live on the right, compiled for real in your browser. This isn't limited to a single class: define helper functions, interfaces, enums, or extra classes (including your own @Injectable() services) above your component, and use inject() or constructor DI to wire them together, exactly like a real Angular file. Every NexaUI component, directive and pipe is available, alongside imports from @angular/core, @angular/common, @angular/forms and rxjs — paste anything from these docs, or build your own, and it will just work.
