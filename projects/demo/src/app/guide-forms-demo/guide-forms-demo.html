Forms

Every form component implements Angular's ControlValueAccessor, so on top of the existing [(value)]/[(checked)] two-way bindings, they also work with [(ngModel)] (template-driven forms) and formControlName/[formControl] (reactive forms) - no adapter, no extra module beyond Angular's own FormsModule/ReactiveFormsModule.
