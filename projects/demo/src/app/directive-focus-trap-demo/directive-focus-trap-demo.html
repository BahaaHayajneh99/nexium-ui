Focus Trap

While nxFocusTrap is true, Tab/Shift+Tab cycling is kept within the host's focusable descendants - Tab from the last one wraps back to the first, and Shift+Tab from the first wraps to the last. Focus is restored to whatever was focused beforehand once the trap deactivates.
