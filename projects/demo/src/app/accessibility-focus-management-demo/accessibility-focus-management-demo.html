Focus Management

When an overlay opens, focus should move into it; while it's open, Tab shouldn't be able to leave it; when it closes, focus should return to whatever opened it.

Try It

Open the modal below, then press Tab repeatedly - focus cycles between the modal's own close button and content instead of leaking out to the rest of the page. Close it and notice focus lands back on the button that opened it.

Current Coverage

nx-modal implements the full pattern: focus moves to its first focusable element on open, Tab/Shift+Tab cycle within it, Escape closes it, and focus is restored to the triggering element on close. nx-dialog, nx-drawer and nx-command-palette don't have the Tab-cycling or focus-restore parts yet - that's a tracked gap, not a hidden one.
